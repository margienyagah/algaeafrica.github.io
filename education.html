AAN EDUCATION HUB

Learning. Conversations. Knowledge Sharing.

The AAN Education Hub brings together webinars, expert perspectives, member contributions and practical knowledge to support learning and collaboration across Africa's growing algae community.

Welcome to the Education Hub

The Education Hub is a space for people to learn from one another, exchange ideas and explore the growing role of algae in Africa. Whether you are a student beginning your journey, a researcher, entrepreneur, educator or professional, the hub provides opportunities to discover new perspectives, participate in conversations and share knowledge with the wider AAN community.

Webinars & Conversations

Explore webinars and expert conversations organised by the Algae Africa Network and its collaborators.

Launching Africa's Algae Movement

Continental Webinar • August 2025

The inaugural Algae Africa Network webinar introduced the vision for a continental algae innovation ecosystem, bringing together researchers, students, entrepreneurs and institutions to discuss collaboration, education and future opportunities.

Scaling Algae Innovation Across Africa

Expert Webinar Series • December 2025

Experts explored how Africa can accelerate algae commercialization through stronger research collaboration, entrepreneurship, value addition and regional partnerships.

Future AAN Conversations

AAN Global Insights

Making algae understandable, visible and actionable across Africa through perspectives from researchers, innovators, entrepreneurs and members of the wider algae community.

FEATURED INSIGHT

Algae Biorefineries and the Future of Sustainable Packaging

By Margaret Nyaga

This concept article explores how integrated algae biorefineries could reduce postharvest food losses by producing biodegradable packaging from locally cultivated algae. The article illustrates how one innovation can connect agriculture, manufacturing and Africa's circular bioeconomy.

AAN RESOURCE LIBRARY

Building Africa's Knowledge Hub for Algae

A growing collection of knowledge, research and practical resources supporting algae science, innovation, education and sustainable development across Africa.

Resource Centre

The Algae Africa Network is building a resource centre that will bring together knowledge contributed by researchers, educators, entrepreneurs, institutions and partners across Africa and beyond. Our aim is to make useful algae knowledge easier to discover, share and apply in African contexts. As the network grows, this library will expand with publications, educational materials, technical resources, case studies and other contributions from the AAN community.

Our Growing Collection

The following areas will form part of the AAN resource collection as materials become available.

Algae Learning Resources

Learning about algae

The AAN Education Hub and Resource Library will gradually develop learning materials covering areas such as algae biology, microalgae, seaweed cultivation, biotechnology, applications and the circular bioeconomy. These resources will be developed and shared as materials become available.
